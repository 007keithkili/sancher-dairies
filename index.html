Our story

Sancher Dairies is a vibrant family‑owned mixed farm in rural Uasin Gishu. We raise healthy cattle, tend thriving bee colonies, and produce rich organic manure — all in harmony with nature. Our days begin early, with fresh milk and honey as our daily gift to the community.

Vision & Mission

Our promise

Vision: To become the most trusted local brand for safe, high-quality dairy & honey.
Mission: Deliver fresh, hygienic, affordable milk & honey to households and institutions across Uasin Gishu County & beyond, while regenerating the land.

Location

Uasin Gishu heartland

Our farm sits on expansive, fertile acres in a rural neighborhood blessed with cool breezes and reliable rainfall — ideal for dairy, apiculture, and nutrient‑rich compost. We hold clear title to the land, ensuring stability for generations.

What we harvest

🥛 Fresh dairy milk

Raw, unprocessed milk from our contented herd. Delivered daily to local families and kiosks — pure, creamy, and full of natural goodness. No additives, no shortcuts.

🍯 Wildcrafted honey

Raw, unfiltered honey from our own beehives. The bees forage on indigenous trees and wildflowers, giving each harvest a unique, rich flavor. Soon available in branded glass jars.

💚 Organic manure

100% natural compost from our zero‑grazing unit. Packed with nutrients, it regenerates soil, improves water retention, and boosts crop yields — loved by farmers across the region.

Stewards of the land
Closed‑loop system

Regenerating soils

Our manure replaces synthetic fertilizers for local farmers, building healthier, carbon‑rich soil across the region. Every scoop returns life to the earth.

Water conservation

Rainwater & efficient use

We harvest rainwater from every rooftop and use low‑waste watering systems, protecting local springs and ensuring our farm thrives even in dry spells.

Pollinator protection

Bee‑friendly practices

No synthetic pesticides near our hives. We plant forage corridors and leave wild edges — creating a sanctuary for bees and other pollinators.

From our farm to your table

The journey of honey

From blossoming acacia trees to the comb, we harvest with care, spin the frames, and bottle raw honey without heating or filtering — preserving every enzyme and drop of flavour.

From cow to compost

Our zero‑grazing system collects manure daily, then ages and turns it into a rich, earthy compost — perfect for vegetables, maize, and fruit trees.

Morning milking ritual

Every morning, our herd is milked under strict hygiene. The milk is cooled immediately and delivered within hours — as fresh as it gets.

Our gentle herd

Healthy, happy cattle

Our cows are a blend of improved breeds, raised with care in a zero‑grazing system. They enjoy nutritious feed, clean water, and regular vet checks — the secret to our premium milk.

Pasture to pail

We grow our own fodder and supplement with mineral blocks. The result is milk with high butterfat and a naturally sweet taste.

The beekeepers' touch

Our apiary is managed by trained local beekeepers. Gentle handling, modern hives, and respect for the bees — that's how we get pure, healthy honey.
