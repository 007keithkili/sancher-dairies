Executive snapshot

Sancher Dairies is a vibrant mixed-farm in rural Uasin Gishu. With 40 cows (26 lactating, 14 heifers), 10 bee hives, and organic manure production, we deliver 150 liters of pure milk daily. Our honey is wild-crafted, our pastures thrive naturally.

Vision & Mission

Our promise

Vision: To become the most trusted local brand for safe, high-quality dairy & honey.
Mission: Deliver fresh, hygienic, affordable milk & honey to households and institutions across Uasin Gishu County & beyond.

Manure → gold

Bee apiary

10 thriving hives produce ~200 kg of pure raw honey per year. Our bees forage on indigenous flora, creating rich, aromatic honey — processed on-site with modern honey equipment.

Manure circular economy

Organic manure from our zero-grazing unit enriches soil & provides extra revenue. Sustainable cycle: happy cows → rich compost → lush forage.

Farm assets

Land title, prime farmland, milking equipment, honey processing gear, and robust infrastructure — building the future of local dairy from the ground up.
