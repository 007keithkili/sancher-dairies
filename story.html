Our story

Rooted in Uasin Gishu, grown with integrity — the journey of Sancher Dairies.

Where it began

A family dream

Sancher Dairies started as a small family farm with a handful of cattle and a few beehives. What drove us was a simple belief: that food should be fresh, honest, and grown without harm to the land. Over the years, we grew — not just in size, but in wisdom.

Generations together

Today, the farm is run by a dedicated team alongside family members. We have learned that the best farming comes from listening — to the animals, to the bees, to the soil. Every season teaches us something new.

Closing the loop

We realized early that waste is just a resource out of place. That's why manure became our gold — transforming cattle waste into rich compost for local farmers. Our farm now operates as a nearly closed system.

The land & the people

Our beekeeping team

Trained local youth manage our apiary with gentle, bee‑centric methods. They've learned modern beekeeping and now mentor others — creating a ripple of skills across the community.

Animal welfare first

Our zero‑grazing system prioritizes cow comfort: soft bedding, clean water, fresh fodder, and regular health checks. Happy cows produce the best milk — that's our philosophy.

Fair & local employment

We hire from the surrounding villages, offering fair wages and training. Many of our team members have grown with the farm, from casual workers to skilled herd managers.

We don't farm to get rich overnight. We farm to leave the land better than we found it — for our children, and for theirs.
Challenges & growth

Drought resilience

We’ve weathered dry seasons by investing in rainwater harvesting, efficient irrigation, and drought‑tolerant forages. Every challenge made us smarter.

From raw milk to value

We started selling only raw milk. But we saw the need for safer, packaged dairy. Today we are building toward pasteurization and branded honey — the next chapter.

Community trust

Over the years, we have earned a reputation for honesty and quality. Our customers know that when they buy from Sancher, they are supporting a farm that cares.

The road ahead

Modern dairy processing

We are preparing to install a small pasteurization and packaging unit. This will allow us to serve schools, shops, and institutions with safe, long‑life milk.

Branded honey

Soon our honey will be available in elegant glass jars, telling the story of our bees and the wildflowers of Uasin Gishu. A product we are proud to put our name on.

Yoghurt & cheese

In the coming years, we plan to launch fresh yoghurt and soft cheeses — turning our rich milk into even more delicious, nutritious products.

Visit us

You are always welcome at Sancher Dairies. Come see the cows, taste the honey, walk the land. We believe in transparency — our gate is open.
